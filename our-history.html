The Birth of Lekham Walauwa

Built over 300 years ago during the Kandyan era, Lekham Walauwa was home to a family of noble status, deeply rooted in Sri Lanka’s royal heritage. Its walls carry whispers of ancient traditions and royal tales.

The architectural style reflects pure Kandyan identity, with hand-carved beams, terracotta tiles, and thick mud walls that kept the home cool and strong through generations.

Legacy & Generations

Passed down through generations, the house saw many changes — from royal feasts to humble family life. Yet, it always remained the heart of tradition in the village, welcoming locals and leaders alike.

Every corner holds the imprint of a different time, from grain storage chambers to secret hideaways built during colonial unrest.

Restoration and Renewal

In 2022, the latest restoration brought new life to the walauwa. Painstaking care was taken to preserve its original design while adding touches of modern comfort — like new bathrooms, electricity, and safe guest spaces.

It now stands not just as a house, but a living museum where every guest becomes part of the story.
