About This Room

Step into the opulent world of the Royal Suite, our most luxurious offering at Lekham Walauwa. This expansive room features exquisite original wooden architecture, intricate carvings, and high ceilings that transport you to a bygone era of Sri Lankan nobility.

The suite is elegantly furnished with a grand four-poster bed, adorned with fine linens and traditional Sri Lankan textiles, ensuring a restful night's sleep. Every detail, from the antique furniture to the subtle lighting, has been carefully curated to enhance your experience of historical luxury.

Large windows invite natural light and offer serene views of our lush, traditional gardens, providing a tranquil escape from the outside world. It's the perfect blend of historical charm and modern comfort.

Experience true grandeur and relaxation in a setting that whispers tales of centuries past. The spacious layout provides ample room for relaxation, whether you're enjoying a book in the cozy sitting area or simply soaking in the ambiance of this unique heritage property.

Private En-suite Bathroom

The Royal Suite boasts a beautifully appointed modern bathroom, complete with all the contemporary conveniences you'd expect. Enjoy a refreshing shower with abundant hot water, surrounded by clean, elegant finishes that complement the suite's heritage feel.

We ensure a pristine and comfortable experience, providing fresh towels and essential toiletries for your convenience. It's a perfect blend of old-world charm and new-world comfort.
