The External Bedroom

About This Room

Step into the External Bedroom, a simple, peaceful space set within a 360-year-old heritage house. This room has been kept close to its original character, so you're staying somewhere with real history rather than a modern hotel room.

The room comes with a comfortable double bed and an attached bathroom. As this is a heritage home rather than a modern hotel, we don't have air conditioning in this room — instead, standing fans are provided to keep you comfortable.

A window looks directly out onto our garden, giving you a calm, green view to wake up to and a genuinely peaceful place to rest.

We're not a five-star hotel, and we don't try to be — what we offer instead is an authentic, quiet stay in a home with three and a half centuries of history behind it. Our aim is to make you comfortable and to look after you well, while keeping the character of the house intact.

Attached Bathroom

The External Bedroom has its own attached bathroom for your privacy and convenience, kept clean and comfortable for your stay.

Fresh towels are provided, and we're happy to accommodate any specific needs you have during your stay — just let us know.
