The Middle Room

About This Room

The Middle Room is a comfortable, simply furnished space within our 360-year-old heritage house. Its window looks directly onto our dining area, and can be closed whenever you'd like more privacy.

The room comes with a comfortable double bed and an attached bathroom. Like the rest of the house, we don't have air conditioning here — a standing fan is provided instead to keep things comfortable.

You'll also find a cupboard for your belongings and a drying rack for clothes, which is handy if you're staying a few nights.

We're not a five-star hotel, and we don't try to be — what we offer is an authentic, comfortable stay in a home with real history, and we do our best to accommodate our guests throughout their time here.

Attached Bathroom

The Middle Room has its own attached bathroom for your privacy and convenience, kept clean and comfortable throughout your stay.

Fresh towels are provided, and we're happy to accommodate any specific needs you have — just let us know.
