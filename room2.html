The Heritage Room

About This Room

Immerse yourself in the authentic charm of the Heritage Room, a beautifully preserved space showcasing traditional Sri Lankan craftsmanship and design. This room offers a cozy and intimate atmosphere, perfect for experiencing the historical essence of Lekham Walauwa.

Furnished with classic wooden pieces and adorned with local art, the Heritage Room provides a unique blend of historical authenticity and serene comfort. You'll find a comfortable queen-size bed, thoughtfully arranged to offer a peaceful retreat after a day of exploration.

The natural light filtering through traditional windows creates a warm and inviting ambiance, allowing you to relax and unwind in a truly distinctive setting.

Every corner of this room tells a story, from its meticulously maintained architecture to the carefully selected decor. It's an ideal choice for guests seeking a deep connection with Sri Lankan history and culture.

Comfortable En-suite Bathroom

The Heritage Room features a clean and modern en-suite bathroom, designed for your comfort and convenience. It includes a refreshing shower with hot water, ensuring a pleasant start or end to your day.

We provide fresh towels and essential toiletries, so you can enjoy your stay without a worry. The thoughtful layout and contemporary fittings make for a comfortable and private experience.
