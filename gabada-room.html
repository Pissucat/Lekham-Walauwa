The Gabada Room

About This Room

The Gabada Room is another quiet corner of our 360-year-old walauwa, kept in its original character rather than fitted out like a modern hotel room. Its window looks out toward the forest side of the property, giving a very different outlook from our garden-facing rooms.

The room comes with an attached bathroom and a drying rack for clothes. As with the rest of the house, we don't have air conditioning here — a standing fan is provided to keep things comfortable.

The forest-facing window brings a cooler, greener outlook, especially pleasant in the early morning.

We're not a five-star hotel, and we don't try to be — what we offer instead is a genuinely peaceful stay inside a home with real history, and we do our best to look after our guests throughout their time here.

Attached Bathroom

The Gabada Room has its own attached bathroom, kept clean and comfortable throughout your stay.

Fresh towels are provided, and we're happy to accommodate any specific needs you have — just let us know.
