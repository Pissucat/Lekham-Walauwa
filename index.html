A Living Piece of History

Welcome to Lekham Walauwa, a magnificent 300-year-old heritage property that has been lovingly restored to offer you an authentic Sri Lankan experience.

Our historic walauwa features 4 beautifully appointed rooms, 3 modern bathrooms, and a traditional kitchen, all while maintaining the authentic charm of ancient Sri Lankan architecture.

Step back in time and experience the grandeur of Sri Lankan nobility while enjoying modern comforts.
